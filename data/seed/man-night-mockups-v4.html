The link

Each notification carries a Google Maps directions URL in its data. Tapping opens it:

https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&destination=Jumbo%27s+Beef+%26+Brew,+3750+N+Woodlawn+Blvd,+Wichita,+KS&destination_place_id=…

iPhone vs Android

iPhone web push has no action buttons, so the whole notification is the link. Android and desktop Chrome also get Directions and Running late buttons.

To test on real phones

Whether tapping opens the Google Maps app or Maps in the browser depends on the phone and whether Google Maps is installed.

Calendar update when the place is set

How it finds the event

Same date, title starting with Man Night @ Eric. Your calendar often has a week entered on both Tuesday and Wednesday: the app updates the event on the night that won, and leaves the other one alone.

If there's no event

It creates one in the same format.

Needs

Write access to the calendar. The secret iCal link is read-only, so the app needs a Google service account added to the Man Night Calendar with “Make changes to events.”

About the data. Places, addresses, visit counts and last-visit dates come from the Man Night calendar (Dec 2011 – Nov 2026, one dinner per week). Map pins come from looking up each address on OpenStreetMap; places without an address were searched by name in Wichita and may be off or missing. Road lines on these maps are approximate and only for orientation; the real app uses Google Maps. “Poplar” and “Poplars” are listed separately, as entered on the calendar. Votes, RSVPs and the noon-vote result are sample data. The web address is a placeholder.
